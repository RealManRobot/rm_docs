# <p class="hidden"> Downloads: </p>Model and Dimensional Drawing

RealMan provides access to a range of downloads, and users can download the documents that they need.

<h1 class='h1Style'>3D model and dimensional drawing</h1>

<p>
This part introduces the 3D model diagram, simulation model, and dimensional drawing of each product. The 3D model diagram is in the general STEP format, supporting the common 3D design software such as SolidWorks, UG, Cero, Pro-E, Rhino and 3Dmax.
</p>
<p>
The simulation model is in URDF format, supporting the use of Gazebo, Moveit, Webots, Matlab, etc. for robotic arm simulation and development.
</p>
<p>
The robotic arm dimensional drawing specifies the outline dimensions, mounting dimensions of base, and the extended dimensions of the end.
</p>
