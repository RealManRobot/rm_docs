# Overview

Realman's blog forum is dedicated to providing a convenient platform for knowledge sharing and interactive communication for robot developers, corporate clients, and technology enthusiasts. By covering a wide range of topics including robotic arms, composite robots, ROS development, and product ecosystems, it aims to help users better understand, utilize, and develop Realman's robotic products. This reduces the barriers to development, enhances efficiency, and offers comprehensive support to businesses and developers.

## Target Audience

1. **Integration Developers and Robotic Arm Application Developers**: Provides typical application cases and development resources based on robotic arms. The blog content covers various development environments and application scenarios to meet the needs of different development settings.
2. **Research Users**: A rich set of API interfaces and typical application demo examples serve as references for research users, aiding them in fully utilizing the functions of robotic arms in their scientific research.
3. **Educational Users**:
   - Design robotic arm experiment courses based on API interfaces and demo examples, enabling students to master the basic use of standard robotic arms.
   - Conduct practical teaching by combining Realman robotic arms with courses such as robotics, robot operating systems, machine vision, and industrial robot operation training, allowing students to apply theoretical knowledge in practice.

## Main Content

### Robotic Arm Related Content

- **Introduction**: Provides a detailed explanation of the hardware structure, interface specifications, and practical operation methods of robotic arms.
- **Version Information**: Regularly updates product version information, introducing new features, improvements, and upgrade considerations.
- **User Manual**: Offers operation guides for robotic arms, including startup, control, and secondary development, ensuring users can use the products correctly and efficiently.
- **FAQ**: Compiles common issues encountered during the use of robotic arms and provides detailed solutions.
- **End Effector Introduction**: Mainly introduces cases and usage methods of Realman's end effectors, including grippers, suction cups, and dexterous hands, helping users understand how to select and apply different end effectors.

### Composite Robot Related Content

- **Introduction**: Explains the application advantages of composite robots, such as combining mobile bases, dexterous hands, head servos, and robotic arms to achieve more flexible operations.
- **Application Case Sharing**: Displays actual application cases of composite robots in industries such as logistics, warehousing, electronics manufacturing, and education.
- **Operation Manual**: Provides detailed operation manuals for the composite robot as a whole and its various accessories, assisting in the correct use of the composite robot.
- **Secondary Development Guidance**: Offers guidance for users with secondary development needs for composite robots, including development interface descriptions, tool recommendations, and development processes, helping to develop functions according to specific requirements.

### ROS Development Related Content

- **Development Case Sharing**: Introduces development examples of Realman's robotic products.
- **Development Guide**: Provides detailed guidance for users with secondary development needs, including development interfaces, tool recommendations, development processes, and solutions to common difficulties encountered during use.

### Training Content

- **Training Information**: Publishes robot training courses organized by Realman, providing related materials and videos for self-learning.

### Ecosystem Related

- **Product Ecosystem**: Introduces the ecological products currently cooperating with Realman and the operation instructions for the compatibility of Realman products with ecological products. This showcases Realman's exploration of new business areas and innovation opportunities with ecological partners, creating greater value for customers.

### Common Function Usage Guide

- Provides detailed usage guides for some common functions, such as quick programming of robotic arms, autonomous navigation and operation of composite robots, and calibration of work and tool coordinate systems, to improve user operation efficiency.
- Through the rich and comprehensive content of the blog forum, Realman hopes to provide the knowledge and assistance needed by different types of users, enhance user experience, and increase Realman's influence and user stickiness in the robotics industry.
